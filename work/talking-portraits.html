PROJECT | 2025

Talking Portraits

An interactive installation that brings historical paintings to life, using AI-generated motion and voices driven by real actors.

  • Project

    Talking Portraits

  • Type

    Interactive Installation

    Generative AI

  • location

    Malmö, Sweden

  • client

    Malmö museum

  • dimensions

    0.7 × 1.2 m

As part of a new exhibition at the Malmö museum, I created an interactive installation that reanimates portrait paintings of the Danish royal family from the 15th century through AI-generated motion, voice, and viewer interaction. The project combines multiple AI tools, including Kling AI and Runway ML to animate photographic reproductions of the original paintings.

To create more nuanced and believable facial expressions, I collaborated with a local acting group and used their performances as a diving input for Runway’s Act-One model. In addition, I recorded the performers reading a script provided by the museum's archivists and used ElevenLabs to transform their voices for each respective historical character.

Portrait 1

King Christian IV

Portrait 2

Portrait 3

Prince Christian

Each portrait is displayed on a vertically mounted 50” Samsung The Frame, enclosed within a classical golden wood frame. A radar sensor mounted behind each frame triggers the animations in response to the presence of a viewer.

The three works were presented as part of the temporary exhibition Makt och Prakt at Malmö museum. From the opening day onward, hundreds of visitors interacted with the portraits throughout the year-long exhibition period. The works were featured in the museum’s marketing campaign, local newspapers, and online publications.
