Pixlar O Ljud Live #1

A real-time audiovisual performance descending from the ocean surface into bioluminescent depths.

  • Project

    Pixlar O Ljud Live #1

  • Type

    Live Performance

    Real-Time Generative Art

  • location

    Malmö, Sweden

  • dimensions

    1920 x 1080 px

  • duration

    20 min

A real-time audiovisual performance developed in TouchDesigner, unfolding as a descent beneath the ocean surface. It begins with gentle waves and a drifting buoy, then dives into vibrant underwater ecosystems. Layer upon layer of audio-reactive effects accumulate, and the recognizable scene gradually dissolves into complex, abstract patterns. Live manipulation of system parameters and sensitivity thresholds brings improvisation into the process, making the performance a unique dialogue between sound, motion, and emergence.

Performed at Pixlar Och Ljud Live, Hypnos Theater, June 2026.
