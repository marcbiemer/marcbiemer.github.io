Pixlar O Ljud Live #2

A real-time audiovisual performance in four chapters, moving from sunset into the depth of night.

  • Project

    Pixlar O Ljud Live #2

  • Type

    Live Performance

    Real-Time Generative Art

  • location

    Malmö, Sweden

  • dimensions

    1920 x 1080 px

  • duration

    30 min

A real-time audiovisual performance developed in TouchDesigner, in which generative visuals respond to live music. Performed together with musician Melisa Hümmet, the work unfolds across four songs. Audio-reactive behaviors let color, light, and form move with the beat, while live adjustments to the system keep the performance open to improvisation. Each scene stands on its own, yet together they form a journey through fading light and deepening night.

Performed at Pixlar Och Ljud Live as part of Malmö Music Innovation Day, Hypnos Theater, September 2026.
