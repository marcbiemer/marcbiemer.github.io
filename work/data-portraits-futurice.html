PROJECT | 2021

Futurice – Data Portraits

Exploring a playful, personal way to connect by turning ordinary postcards into generative portraits of their senders.

  • Project

    Futurice – Data Portraits

  • Type

    Data Art

    Digital Print

  • location

    Stockholm, Sweden

  • client

    Futurice

  • dimensions

    14.8 × 10.5 cm

I was commissioned by Futurice’s Head of Marketing Sweden to develop a playful and personal way to engage their local business contacts. The project proposed transforming ordinary postcards into data portraits of their sender. A questionnaire of nine concise questions was developed, focusing on the subject’s professional profile and working methods. I developed graphical metaphors and analogies to represent the data in an intriguing way as the postcard’s front motif. Each design was generated programmatically using a custom Processing script.

Each participant received their data portrait as both a physical print and a digital asset. The project was featured in various online publications (e.g. Futurice blog and arkitektkopia blog), and presented at the WeAreDevelopers World Congress (Berlin, 2022) and the UPPSTART Conference (Stockholm, 2021). In addition to engaging clients and business partners, the data portraits also functioned as effective conversation starters within Futurice itself.
