Marc Biemer
b.94 | GER

Biography

Marc Biemer is a new media artist working at the intersection of art, data, and technology. His practice explores the tension between structure and instability through generative systems. Working across digital and physical formats, Marc has a particular interest in data sculptures and the embodiment of data through interactive installations, immersive experiences, and live performances.
